School fees payment policy

Zannoo International School is a cashless school. We kindly urge parents to settle outstanding fees with the accounts office promptly, to ensure uninterrupted learning.

Full or 75% of the total bill should be paid on or before reopening.

Management has adopted a structured and predictable fee adjustment policy:

  • School fees are adjusted once every academic year, in Second Term.
  • Once adjusted, fees remain unchanged until the Second Term of the following academic year.
  • For the calendar year, any adjustment made in September applies consistently throughout the year until the following September, before another review may be considered.

This approach is meant to give parents and the school stability, transparency and advance planning.

Feeding fees

  • Approved feeding fees are communicated to parents at the beginning of each term if there has been an adjustment.
  • Feeding fees are calculated termly.
  • Feeding fees must be paid in full.
  • Payment is made through the school’s MoMo account.
