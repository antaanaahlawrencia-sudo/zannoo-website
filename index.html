About us

Welcome to Zannoo International School

The school of life and learning: a beacon of holistic quality education in the Upper West Region of Ghana.

Zannoo International School combines the Oxford International Curriculum with the GES curriculum. Our approach is skills-based and inquiry-led, with a focus on critical thinking and global competence, so learners are globally competitive while firmly grounded in national standards and cultural values.

We opened on Saturday 6th September 2025 on the conviction that where a child lives should never limit access to quality education.
