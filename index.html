Our campus

A bright school in the heart of Wa

Zannoo International School stands at New Nakore-Paani in Wa, bringing international-standard learning to the Upper West Region, with inclusive, tech-enhanced spaces, an infirmary with a professional nurse and a safe, caring environment for every child.
