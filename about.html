About Us

A beacon of holistic quality education in Ghana.

Who we are

Zannoo International School (ZIS) is a school in Ghana’s Upper West Region, at New Nakore-Paani in Wa, that combines the Oxford International Curriculum with the GES curriculum. Our motto, The School of Life and Learning, reflects how we teach: academic excellence, Godly values and a commitment to greatness belong together.

Our approach is skills-based and inquiry-led, with a focus on critical thinking and global competence, so that learners are globally competitive while remaining firmly grounded in national educational standards and cultural values.

Our story

ZIS began operations after its successful opening on Saturday 6th September 2025, and our first term began on Tuesday 9th September 2025. We started with about 37 learners and now have over 200.

The school was founded on a deep conviction that geographical location should never limit access to quality education. Zannoo International School exists to bridge the educational opportunity gap by giving learners in the Upper West Region the same high-quality learning experiences available in major educational hubs such as Greater Accra and Greater Kumasi.

Safety, safeguarding and compliance

Safeguarding, health and child protection are a priority at ZIS. We have an infirmary with a professional nurse at post and all safety protocols in place.

The school is registered with GES, NaSIA, Social Welfare, the District Assembly (business permit), Fire Service, EPA, SSNIT and GRA, and holds full accreditation from the Oxford International Curriculum for all levels, from Early Years to A/O-Level.
