Admissions

Join a school where learning goes beyond the classroom. Here is how to apply.

How to apply

  1. Send an enquiryFill in the form below, call, or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Visit the schoolTour the campus and meet our staff.
  3. Submit the applicationCollect the form from the office and return it with the documents listed here.
  4. Assessment and interviewYour child meets our teachers for a friendly, age-appropriate assessment.
  5. Offer and enrolmentSuccessful applicants receive an offer letter and complete enrolment by paying the admission fees.

Dress code

Monday, Tuesday and Thursday

School uniform (regular uniform).

Wednesday

School polo shirt with khaki skirt (girls) or shorts (boys).

Friday

School sportswear (P.E.).

Smart today, successful tomorrow.

School rules

  • Learners must look clean and tidy always.
  • Uniforms must be ironed always.
  • Prescribed footwear is black footwear.
  • Fingernails and hair must be well kept.
  • No fizzy drinks in school.
  • Bring all required books and materials daily.
  • Be punctual and arrive on time every day.
  • Show respect to teachers, staff and fellow learners.
  • Keep the school clean and use the bins provided.

Documents to bring

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school report or transcript
  • Transfer or leaving certificate (if applicable)
  • Immunisation record
  • Parent or guardian ID
