Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents.

How do I apply?

Send an enquiry on the admissions page, visit the school, collect and return the application form, attend a friendly assessment, then receive an offer letter and complete enrolment.

Which classes do you offer?

We teach the Oxford International Curriculum alongside the GES curriculum. Pre-School has the Baby Unit, Creche, Nursery 1 and 2, and Reception 1 and 2. Then Key Stage 1 (Years 1 and 2), Key Stage 2 (Years 3 to 6) and Key Stage 3 (Year 7). Please call the office to confirm which classes are open for admission.

What age can my child start?

It depends on your child’s age and level. Please call us on 050 477 7497 and we will advise the right class for your child.

Which curriculum do you follow?

The Oxford International Curriculum alongside the GES curriculum, so learners are globally competitive while staying grounded in national standards. ZIS holds full Oxford International Curriculum accreditation.

How big are the classes?

A maximum of 20 learners per class. SEN support is available.

How do you look after children’s health and safety?

Safeguarding, health and child protection are a priority. We have an infirmary with a professional nurse at post, written safeguarding and health and safety policies, and weekly mindfulness and well-being sessions.

What documents do I need?

The completed application form, birth certificate copy, passport-size photographs, previous school report, transfer or leaving certificate (if applicable), immunisation record and parent or guardian ID.

Can I visit before applying?

Yes. We encourage it. Contact the office or use the tour option on the enquiry form.

What is the school uniform?

Monday, Tuesday and Thursday: regular school uniform. Wednesday: school polo shirt with khaki skirt (girls) or shorts (boys). Friday: school sportswear. Footwear is black. See the dress code.

How do I pay fees?

We are a cashless school. Pay by MTN or Telecel Mobile Money, or by bank deposit or transfer. Full or 75% of the bill is due on or before reopening. See how to pay.

How much are the fees?

See our fees and key dates page, or call 050 477 7497 / 024 498 4974 for the current fee structure.

Can my child join from another school?

Yes. Bring the previous school report and transfer or leaving certificate, and your child will have a short assessment so we place them in the right class.

How can I contact the school quickly?

Call or message us on WhatsApp on 050 477 7497, or email zannoo.int.sch@gmail.com.

Still have a question?

Contact us and we will gladly help.
