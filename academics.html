Academics

The Oxford International Curriculum alongside the GES curriculum, with a clear progression at every stage.

Our curriculum

Zannoo International School deliberately offers an international curriculum alongside the GES curriculum, so that learners are globally competitive while remaining firmly grounded in national standards and cultural values. This choice reflects our commitment to equity, excellence and global relevance for learners in the Upper West Region.

Oxford International Curriculum

Taught across the school, from Pre-School to Key Stage 3, with skills-based, inquiry-led instruction and a clear developmental progression.

GES curriculum

Aligned with Ghana Education Service standards, so learning stays grounded in national requirements.

Skills for the world

A focus on inquiry, critical thinking and global competence.

ZIS holds full accreditation from the Oxford International Curriculum for all levels, from Early Years to A/O-Level.

Learning environment

Small classes

A maximum of 20 learners per class.

Inclusive, tech-enhanced spaces

Classrooms designed so every learner can take part.

SEN support

Support is available for learners with special educational needs.

Well-being and whole-school approach

Mindfulness and SEL

Weekly mindfulness and social-emotional learning sessions.

Trained staff

Staff training on well-being and mental health.

Clear policies

Anti-bullying, inclusion and other policies that keep every child safe and included.

Teacher and student support

Qualified teachers

Teachers meet Oxford International Curriculum standards (PGCE, with English proficiency at B2+/C1+).

Differentiated instruction

Lessons adapted to the needs of each learner.

ESL support

English-as-a-second-language support, with proficiency tracking.

Ongoing development

Structured continuous professional development for staff, led by our OIC team and academic team, with feedback for quality assurance.

Our 3-year plan

Year 1: Foundation

Enrol learners for the Oxford International Curriculum from Early Years to Primary 6, with parental orientation and learning outcomes, assessment and interventions.

Year 2: Expansion

Integrate STEAM in Primary and Key Stage 3 (Lower Secondary), as an ICT-based school.

Year 3: Consolidation

Student-led project-based learning, online learning to support classes, and a stronger partnership with parents.
